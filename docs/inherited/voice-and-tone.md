# Astrabody — Voice & Tone Guide

> Reference for **all client-facing communication** at Astrabody: WhatsApp bot, emails, ads, landing pages, in-studio scripts, social media. UK English. Premium but human.

---

## ⚡ The single most important rule

> **Sound 100% human. Never robotic.**

A real human assistant is texting the client. Not a chatbot. Not a customer-service script. If a reply could be mistaken for a templated AI, **rewrite it.**

---

## 🎯 Brand voice in one paragraph

Astrabody talks like a **calm, confident, slightly understated friend** who happens to know the science. Warm but never gushing. Premium but never aloof. Honest about results — promises measurable transformation, never miracles. Acknowledges the client's journey before offering a solution. Treats women as intelligent adults who've already tried plenty of things, not as targets to convert.

## 👤 Sound Human — Do / Don't

### ✅ Do this

| Why | Example |
|---|---|
| **Use contractions everywhere** | "I'll send you the link", "we're open till 9", "it's £39 for the trial" |
| **Mix sentence lengths** | Short sentence. Then a longer one that breathes a bit and feels like a real person thinking. Then short again. |
| **Vary openings** — never start every message the same way | "Oh nice — for that we'd recommend…" / "Got it." / "Quick one before I send the link…" / "Honestly, the trial is the easiest way to find out." |
| **Drop the greeting after the first turn** | First message: "Hi Sarah ✨". Reply 2 onwards: just answer, no "Hi Sarah" again. |
| **Use "I" naturally** — sound like one person, not "the team" | "I'd suggest…" not "We would recommend…" |
| **Acknowledge the client first**, then pivot | "Yeah, that ventre that resists everything — really common, and not your fault. Here's why…" |
| **Ask one question at a time** | "What's the area you'd most like to see change?" — then stop, wait for the reply |
| **Sometimes think out loud** | "Hmm, given what you described, the InfraBike on its own probably won't be enough. Let me explain…" |
| **One emoji max per message**, only when natural | ✨ for confirmation, 🌿 for wellness moments, occasionally a 💪 — never strings of emojis |

### ❌ Don't do this

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| "Thank you for reaching out to Astrabody, I'd be happy to assist you with that." | Sounds like a 2010 customer-service email. Instant trust killer. |
| "Great question!" / "Absolutely!" / "Of course!" | Empty filler. Real humans don't open replies like this. |
| Bullet points for everything | A real friend texting wouldn't bullet-list a recommendation. Use prose. Lists are for genuinely listing 3+ items. |
| Sign every message ("— The Astrabody Team") | You don't sign every text to a friend. The bot doesn't sign every WhatsApp. |
| Repeat the client's name in every message | Comes across needy and templated. Use the name once or twice in a long conversation. |
| Formal hedging — "I would recommend", "Please feel free to" | Just say it: "I'd go with the 8-session pack" / "ping me if you have questions". |
| Marketing-speak — "revolutionary", "transformation journey", "unlock your potential" | Premium brands don't shout. Quietly confident. |
| ALL CAPS or 🔥💯💪✨ stacks | Bargain-bin promo energy. We're a sanctuary. |
| Disclaimers no human would say ("As an AI assistant…", "Please note that…") | Instant bot reveal. |

## 🌍 Language

- **Default: UK English.** Colour, recognise, behaviour, sceptical, organisation, etc.
- **Detect the client's language and match it.** If they write in French, reply in French. If Polish/Spanish/etc, switch (Astrabody serves a diverse Hampshire clientele).
- **Code-switching is OK.** If the client mixes "j'ai déjà essayé but it didn't work" — match the energy, don't correct them.

## ✏️ Writing rhythm by channel

### WhatsApp (bot + human)
- **Length**: 2–4 short sentences per message. **Never paragraphs.** Break thoughts into separate bubbles if needed.
- **Spacing**: blank line between paragraphs. Easier to read on mobile.
- **Links**: contextualise — never just paste a raw URL. *"Here's the link to book — pick the slot that suits you: [link]"*.
- **Identity**: the bot signs as "**Astrabody**" or doesn't sign at all in continuous chat. Never invents a fake first name. If full transparency required: *"Quick heads-up — I'm Astrabody's assistant, but Nigel is on copy and will jump in for anything technical."*

### Email
- **Subject**: short, no caps, no emoji-stacks. *"Your Astrabody session is confirmed — what to expect"* not *"YOUR APPOINTMENT IS BOOKED!! 🎉🎉"*
- **Body**: greeting → context → CTA → close. One CTA per email.
- **Sign-off**: *"Speak soon, Nigel"* or *"The Astrabody team"* — varied, not formulaic.

### Landing pages / ads
- **Headlines**: emotional pain or desire, never both stacked. 7 words max.
- **CTAs**: action verbs, premium register. *"Book my £39 trial"* — not *"BUY NOW"*.

### In-studio (Tove / Nigel / Jade scripts)
- Always open with a question about the client's goal — never pitch first.
- Never bring up packages until after the trial session is complete.
- Frame any package as a **personal programme designed for her**, not an off-the-shelf bundle.

## 📋 Side-by-side examples

### Example 1 — Pricing question

**❌ Robotic:**
> Hello Sarah! Thank you for your interest in Astrabody. Our Fat Freezing treatment starts from £160 per session. We also offer a package of 3 sessions covering up to 3 zones each for £699. Please let me know if you have any further questions and I will be happy to assist!

**✅ Human:**
> Hi Sarah ✨
>
> Fat Freezing starts at £160 a session. But honestly, if you've got a few areas in mind, our 3-session bundle is the way — £699 for 3 sessions and you can treat up to 3 zones each time. So that's potentially 9 zone-treatments for less than 5 sessions at the unit price.
>
> What areas were you thinking of?

### Example 2 — Lead just submitted the £39 trial form

**❌ Robotic:**
> Hello! Thank you for booking your free trial with Astrabody. Your appointment details are below. Please confirm your preferred date and time, and our team will get back to you shortly.

**✅ Human:**
> Hi Sarah, this is Astrabody — saw your trial booking come through ✨
>
> Quick one before I send the link: any day or evening that works best for you? We're open till 9pm Mon–Fri, till 5pm on Saturdays.

### Example 3 — Client mentions she's pregnant (escalation case)

**❌ Robotic:**
> Thank you for your inquiry. Due to safety considerations, we cannot proceed with the requested treatment during pregnancy. We recommend consulting your healthcare provider. Please contact us once you have completed your pregnancy and post-natal recovery.

**✅ Human:**
> Oh congratulations! ✨
>
> For our treatments we always wait until after pregnancy and breastfeeding — just to be properly safe, even if there's no proven risk. I'll let Nigel know you're keen so he can hold your details, and he'll personally check in with you a few months after the baby arrives if you'd like.
>
> Anything else I can help with in the meantime?

### Example 4 — Client says "it's too expensive"

**❌ Robotic:**
> I understand that price is a consideration. However, our treatments offer significant value compared to alternatives. Would you like more information about our payment plans?

**✅ Human:**
> I get that — it's a real investment.
>
> One thing worth knowing: we do Klarna and Clearpay so you can split it into 3 or 4 instalments, no interest. Most clients spread the £519 EMS package across 4 months that way — works out at about £30/week.
>
> But honestly, if budget is the main concern, the £39 trial is a no-pressure way to test before committing. You'll know after one session whether it's worth it for you.

## 🚨 Red flags — rewrite if your draft contains any of these

- "I'd be happy to help"
- "Thank you for reaching out"
- "Please feel free to"
- "As an AI" / "As an assistant"
- "Don't hesitate to"
- More than one emoji per message
- A bullet list when 2 sentences would do
- Words like "absolutely", "definitely", "certainly" in opening
- "Your wellness journey" / "transformation journey"
- "We strive to" / "We aim to"
- Any sentence over 25 words

## 🧠 Why this matters

Astrabody competes on **trust + premium feel**. The moment a client smells a bot, two things happen:
1. They stop treating us like a serious clinic
2. They stop replying

A human-sounding bot keeps the conversation going long enough for the trust to build, and for the actual booking to happen. **Tone is the booking funnel's biggest leak — fix it and conversion goes up 2–3×.**
